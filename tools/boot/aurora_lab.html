The model

Each curtain is a thin vertical sheet hanging along a wandering curve (sines plus noise folds drifting over time). A ray from the eye is marched only through the slab around each sheet. Light peaks just above a sharp lower edge and fades upward; the colour follows altitude (violet fringe, green body, red top, like oxygen 557.7 nm and 630 nm). Vertical rays are noise along the curtain. It all adds as light, then glow and filmic tone mapping.

Two planes, as on the machine

The aurora plane is the one we bake offline and play back. The star plane is live: the stars, their flight and the falcon are a few hundred points and lines a frame, cheap on ZIGGY. Load your own night photo: its stars are extracted, painted out of the picture, and become the flying points. The falcon is our own placeholder model.
