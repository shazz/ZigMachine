# Kestrel Racing M2: driving (R82)

Cart 160 `racer`, on top of M1 (`M1.md`). M2 ports freds72's car physics (MIT; `LICENSE_NOTE.md`) to Zig on PICO-8's 16.16
numbers, drives a car round the oval with keyboard and joystick, draws it with three POVs and skidmarks, and adds
benchmark **lap v1**. Spec: `prototypes/racer_re/PORT_PLAN.md` section 5, row M2.

## The physics bar: bit for bit

`apps/zig/scenes/racer/car.zig` runs `steer`, `prepare`, `integrate`, `update` and the player's `control` in the original's
order at a 30 Hz tick; `ground.zig` has `is_inside`, `find_face`, `face_collide`; `seg.zig` has `make_track` (the track
position u / v). The numbers are `fix.zig`: PICO-8's 16.16 (wrapping add, `(a*b)>>16`, saturating truncating divide, the
positive modulo, the abacus square root) and its integer `sin` / `cos` / `atan2`.

**Result: exact.** Against the Lua reference (z8lua + freds72's engine, `tools/racer/ref_harness.py`) the port matches every
column of every tick, with no tolerance, on:

| run | ticks | what it covers |
|---|---|---|
| `lap` (the harness' autopilot, recorded as buttons) | 1,500 | a **whole lap** (segment 120 is passed at tick ~1,390), the crest and the banked turn, hundreds of sliding ticks |
| `wall` (accelerate + right) | 600 | the outer wall: 51 hits, the sliding contact and the direct hit |

Per tick: position, velocity, angle, face id, track u and v, the wall-hit count, the wheel turn, the steering angle and the
slide flag. `sin`, `cos` over all 65,536 angles, `atan2` over a 301 x 301 grid and `x^0.5` over 32,768 values are pinned by
CRC against z8lua (`tools/racer/fix_pin.py`; the pins are in `fix_test.zig`).

How the reference reaches the cart without freds72's files: `tools/racer/phys_fixture.py` runs the local-only harness and
writes `apps/zig/assets/screens/racer/phys_lap.bin` / `phys_wall.bin` (per-tick raw state, our track, our inputs) and
`lap_v1.btn` (the lap's buttons, 360 ticks, 360 bytes, the only one the cart embeds). `tools/tests/test_racer_phys.py`
fails if those files, or the Zig CRC pins, differ from a fresh z8lua run (skipped without z8lua).

**The trig tables are ours.** z8lua's `sin` / `atan2` are table lookups (4,097 and 2,049 entries). Both tables are exactly
`round(sin(2 pi i / 16384) * 65536) - 16 i` and `round(atan(i / 2048) / (2 pi) * 65536)`, so `fix.zig` builds them at compile
time from a formula and copies no table (the open "trigtables.h provenance" point of M0 is closed: nothing is copied).

`--break slip`: key `Z` / `car.break_slip` moves the front-tyre slip threshold 0.22 -> 0.25. The slide flag (a column of the
reference) and the lap's slide count change, the CRC list changes: `racer_drive.mjs --break slip` and the native test fail.

### Deliberate differences from the Lua

- A position **off the 32 x 32 grid** has no ground (`find_face` returns nothing). The Lua's `x | z << 5` key aliases onto another tile.
- A degenerate (flat) segment walk is bounded by the ring length (the Lua recurses).
- The NPC physics (`make_npc`, the PID, `update_contacts`) is M3; `steer` keeps the `braking` argument they use.

## The track binary is version 2

`trackc.py` writes: the **border mask** in each face record's old pad (bit k = edge k is a wall edge, ascending, refused
otherwise), a last block with the racing line's **left / right vertex ids** (the physics' segment quads), the grid pose's
**segment** in its old pad, three palette ramps (12 car body, 13 dark: tyres and skidmarks, 14 glass; 15 ramps now). The
cart refuses version 1. The committed `oval.ktrk` / `city.ktrk` are regenerated (the M1 view pins did not move). M1.md's
"track binary" section describes version 1.

## Driving and the render

- **Tick and pose** (`drive.zig`): every second `update()` by count runs a tick; the tick's frame shows the midpoint of the
  previous and current pose, the next frame the current one (one tick of latency, 33 ms at most, plus the frame).
- **POVs** (key `v`): far chase, near chase, cockpit (the M1 offsets), lerped over 30 ticks. The cockpit sits in the car,
  does not draw the body and sets the near plane to **0.05** (`clip.near`, a per-frame value; the default 0.25 and every M1
  pin are untouched; `drive_test.zig` clips at 0.05).
- **The car** (`carmodel.zig`, `carview.zig`): our own model, six boxes (body, cabin, four wheel blocks), built at compile
  time in the track's face-record layout so the track's transform / clip / fill draw it; the camera's view is recomposed for
  the car's space. Light is baked from each face's normal (it does not turn with the car). The wheels do not spin or steer yet.
- **Painter's order** (`project.zig`, `frame.zig`): car and skid polygons are pushed between `collect` and `sort` into the
  same bucket sort. A car face's key is its own mean depth, never below the key of the ground face under the car (so a long road
  quad cannot paint over it); a skidmark takes its road face's key and follows it. `frame.faceKey()` records the key per face.
  A car is not sorted per face against walls or crests beyond that (M3 has seven of them).
- **Skidmarks** (`skid.zig`): the original's emitters (a mark every 10 ticks or on a face change, the leading edge stretched
  between), 4 wheels, a ring of 24 marks, 60 ticks of life, drawn as quads 0.04 wide (the original's 0.01 is below the Q7 grid).
- **Input**: the host's arrows (`input` / `inputRelease`: up, down, left, right) and the board's joystick (fire = accelerate).
  Keys: `n` drive on / off, `b` lap v1, `v` POV, `r` / Enter back to the grid, `Z` the slip break. M1's keys are unchanged; `l`
  returns to lap v0. Driving is on the oval only (the stress city has no start for it).

## Lap v1 (the benchmark's input stream)

Key `b`: the car on the grid, the Lua lap's buttons from tick 90, 360 ticks = **720 frames**, then the grid again. It prints
what lap v0 prints (the CRC every 60 frames, the lap summary) and:

```
racer,phys,<ticks>,<px>,<py>,<pz>,<angle>,<u>,<hits>,<slides>     raw 16.16, every 60 frames (= the reference's row 88 + ticks)
racer,physus,<ticks>,<avg us a tick>,<max us>                      at the lap's end (hostMicros; 0 headless)
```
Pinned in `racer_drive.mjs`: `a0944986 17cfd636 b7c64482 89e23ed1 8d51bfd6 8bed842c 7223d1c7 453fb026 25141122 072c2821 f9536170 9b438a44`
(oval, R = 5, CPU fill, mulsh, far POV); the AFFINE fill prints the same list. The physics lines equal the Lua at the same tick.
**Changed in M3:** the last three entries were `569ee5f8 132d82b0 713d6984` until the car's transform stopped overflowing
beyond 64 m from the origin (M3.md); the list above is current.

## Costs (estimates; no board run yet)

Physics, a rough op count (not measured): one tick is a few hundred fixed-point multiplies and about a dozen divides and two
square roots (the `sin` / `cos` are table reads), plus a `normz` per face tested on a face change: **well under 0.05 Mc a
tick**, one tick every second frame: ~0.02 Mc a frame. The car adds up to 30 faces and 48 vertices a frame
(~0.03 Mc by the M1 per-vertex model), skidmarks at most 24 quads. `apps/harness/racer_costs.mjs` was not re-run for the car
(left, below). `racer,physus` prints the measured tick on the board.
