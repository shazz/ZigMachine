# Kestrel Racing M1: the renderer spike (R82)

Cart 160 `racer` (disk `demo-racer.zmd`). A view-only cart: a camera flies an original track along its racing
line (the benchmark's **lap v0**), through freds72's tile / PVS / painter pipeline in Q16 integers + ZM-X `mulsh`.
Spec: `prototypes/racer_re/PORT_PLAN.md` section 5, row M1. Licence: `LICENSE_NOTE.md`. Files: `apps/zig/scenes/racer.zig`
and `apps/zig/scenes/racer/`, the converter `tools/racer/trackc.py`, the Python reference `tools/racer/ref_render.py`.

## What it does, per frame

1. **Camera** (`cam.zig`): lap v0 is a script indexed by FRAME COUNT (720 frames a lap, the three POVs in thirds),
   never by the clock: the same frame is the same picture on any machine. Fixed poses A-H (eighths of the lap).
2. **Visibility** (`pvs.zig`): the baked PVS of the camera's tile and yaw bucket (16 buckets), cut at the draw
   distance R. The runtime DDA is the fallback for a tile the converter did not bake, and the test oracle.
3. **Collect** (`frame.zig`, `xform.zig`, `clip.zig`): per tile, per face not yet seen: back-face test (dual-sided
   skips it), vertex transform through a frame-stamped cache (nine `mulsh16` a vertex), trivial reject by outcode,
   Sutherland-Hodgman against the near plane and a guard band when a vertex needs it, projection (`rcp`, two
   `mulsh16` a coordinate), depth key. A face's decals follow it with its key.
4. **Sort**: a stable counting sort on 1,024 buckets of 1/8 unit, far to near.
5. **Fill** (`poly.zig`, `sinks.zig`): ONE span emitter (sub-pixel Q4 edges, each edge walked from its upper end)
   feeds either the **CPU** sink (aligned word stores) or the **AFFINE** sink (24-byte records, a flat texture of
   the 256 palette indices, no colour key; a full half of a 2 x 2,048-span ring is one CHISEL op).
6. **Sky and backdrop** (`draw.zig`, `backdrop.zig`): sky and ground rows are the first spans of the stream (the sky
   is palette entry 1, rewritten per line by `zg.copper`, a real raster); the backdrop is one keyed AFFINE op
   (a 1024 x 48 strip, 48 spans). One plane, two pages: the shown page is the enabled one.
7. **Benchmark mode v0** (`bench.zig`, `report.zig`, `hud.zig`): per-stage `hostMicros` counters, 32-frame windows,
   the OSD band (O), CSV lines on the console, a CRC-32 of the view every 60 lap frames and a lap summary.

## Keys

`3`-`8` draw distance R | `f` fill path: CPU / AFFINE | `m` mulsh instruction / plain twin | `g` vertex transform in
f32 (the A/B of the plan) | `d` decals on/off (the "LOD" knob of M1: the cart has one detail layer) | `t` oval / stress city |
`p` pause | `o` benchmark OSD | `u` show the derived "uncapped" figure | `A`-`H` a fixed pose | `l` back to the lap |
Esc quits. `X` / `Y` are the harness' `--break edge` / `--break clip` switches.

## Console lines (what the board run and the browser both print)

```
racer,csv,<frame>,<track>,<R>,<cpu|affine>,<MULSH|PLAIN|F32>,<tiles>,<faces>,<verts>,<polys>,<spans>,<pixels>,<vis>,<collect>,<sort>,<sky>,<fill>,<hud>,<avg us>,<min us>,<max us>,<fps10>
racer,crc,<lap frame>,<crc32 of the view rows 12..187>        every 60 lap frames
racer,lap,<frames>,<track R fill mul>,<avg us>,<max us>,<xor of the crcs>
```
Stage microseconds are 32-frame averages; the headless harness answers `hostMicros` with 0 (its lines carry the
counts and the CRCs). The oval's lap CRC list at R = 5, CPU fill, mulsh, pinned in `racer_headless.mjs`:
`6d55d849 bff1c7be a9f66c2b e3bfc562 3ea0fc9b fb56cdc3 0630972a 0f744af9 9b52f6ef 7f8d7500 a994fa26 62a4919a`.
A board run at the same knobs must print the same list (the f32 transform is NOT in it: it moves edge pixels).

## The track binary (`.ktrk`)

`tools/racer/trackc.py in.ktrk.json -o out.ktrk` (stdlib only, `python3 -I`). 128-byte header, palette ramps (RGB),
i16 vertices (1/128 unit), 32-byte face and inner-face records (plane `n` Q14, `cp` Q21, colour = 16 + ramp * 8 +
baked light), tile face lists, the 16 x 33 ray directions, the baked PVS (R = 8, far to near, for the camera tiles
only: the racing line's tiles +-1), the racing line (centre Q16, yaw turns) and the start grid. CRC-32 over the body;
the cart (`trk.zig`) refuses a bad magic, version, CRC, size or block bound, and validates every index it will
follow. `apps/zig/assets/screens/racer/{oval,city}.ktrk` are committed (240 KB and 755 KB);
`tools/tests/test_racer_trackc.py` fails if they differ from what the generators and the converter write today.

**Reading.**
- **Oval: the bar is met with room.** 1.12-1.30 Mc at R = 3..8 (60 fps at every R, including R = 8). FRESCO's one plane
  is 0.7 of it: the cart's own share is 0.40-0.58 Mc, the low end of PORT_PLAN 2.3's cart column (0.43-0.87 at R = 5).
- **Stress city: the bar is NOT met in the estimate.** R = 8 is 5.65 Mc (~18 fps); 30 fps holds to R = 4 (2.99 Mc). The
  cart is 4.9 Mc at R = 8: **collect** (cull, transform, clip, project) 2.8 Mc and **fill** 1.6 Mc. The collect stage
  is cache-hostile (20% D$ misses on the face and vertex-cache loads) and bills 15 `mulsh` a transformed vertex (nine
  for the view, two for `rcp`, four for the projection). The stress city is the worst case on purpose (81 faces a tile,
  every tile populated); the decision rule of PORT_PLAN 2.3 applies. Not tried in M1: a per-tile frustum test before
  the faces are submitted (2,192 submitted for 743 drawn), a slope from `rcp` instead of a `div` an edge, merging the
  coplanar faces of a block in the converter, a smaller vertex-cache entry (24 B now).
- **AFFINE vs CPU fill:** AFFINE saves 0.08-0.35 Mc of cart time (the pixel stores) and the estimate adds ~0.05 Mc for
  the CHISEL wait; the totals are within 1-4% of each other. **The estimate cannot decide it**: AFFINE's per-pixel and
  per-span cost on the board is exactly the unmeasured part (PORT_PLAN 2.2). The board A/B decides.
- **mulsh / plain / f32:** the model puts f32 at +0.01-0.38 Mc over plain; `frame_cost` cannot see the one-op saving of
  the ZM-X instruction (it bills the import as a call), so measure `m` and `g` on the board.
- **Stage split (hot regions >= 0.01 Mc):** oval R = 5: collect 0.19, fill 0.14, sort 0.02, sky rows and the rest 0.09,
  `mulsh` bodies 0.04. City R = 8: collect 2.81, fill 1.64, sort 0.04, `mulsh` bodies 0.75.


## What was cut or changed from the plan

- **Plan cuts:** the real **uncapped** mode (a cart cannot skip the VBL wait today: the OSD / CSV show the derived
  `1e6 / avg us`); the **serial path** is `zg.Console.log`, unverified on the board; **LOD** is the decals layer
  (the track has no other detail layer); the cockpit POV's `z_near` 0.05 is M2 (here 0.25 for all three POVs).
- **Changed:** the clip also has a **guard band** (|x|, |y| <= 3 z) so every projected vertex stays in the edge
  walker's range; the sort key is the **mean** view depth (not freds72's `ni / (y^2 + z^2)`), kept by decals;
  the PVS is "R DDA steps" over 33 rays and a 3 x 3 always-on neighbourhood (`pvs_bake.py` has the definition);
  PVS is baked for the camera tiles only (the line's tiles +-1; others use the DDA); the **plane** count is one
  with two pages, as planned.
- **Not in M1 by design:** physics, cars, HUD game states, music, audio (M2-M4).

## To measure on the board (for the lead; `board_fps.py` + the OSD, a cart built `ZM_ZMX=1`)

1. **The pass bar:** >= 60 fps at R = 5 on the oval; >= 30 fps at R = 8 on the stress city (`t`). The estimates above
   say the oval passes with room and the stress city does not (see the table): record the real numbers.
2. **Both fill paths** (`f`) at R = 3..8 on both tracks, from the pose E / D heaviest frames (keys `E`, `D`, after `t`
   for the city) and along the lap: frame time and `missed` VBLs from the probe, the OSD's per-stage microseconds.
   The M1 decision (CPU or AFFINE fill) is the lower board frame time at R = 5.
3. **`mulsh` vs plain vs f32** (`m`, `g`): the per-vertex saving; the `collect` stage's microseconds.
4. **Console CSV on the board:** does `zg.Console.log` reach the serial console / glass? If not, a glass "bench log"
   service is its own ROADMAP row (plan 2.5). Capture the `racer,lap` summary and the CRC list: the list must equal
   the pinned one above (equal knobs).
5. **Uncapped:** can a cart skip the VBL wait (host / ROM)? Today the figure is derived.
6. **The CPU path's backdrop sync:** in CPU mode the backdrop is an async CHISEL op into the same plane and the cart
   calls `blitSync` before its stores. The browser blitter is synchronous, so only the board can show an overdraw.
7. **Copper sky on both pages** (one entry rewritten per line, installed on both planes; no gradient on one page
   would be a flip-timing issue); `zg.mem` after init (the cart logs `cart RAM free`: 625 KB in the browser).
8. **Re-fit** PORT_PLAN 2.3's per-item costs from the stage microseconds and the counts in the CSV.
