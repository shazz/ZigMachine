# Kestrel Racing M4: audio (R82)

M4 gives the racer its music and its sound effects. The effects needed a new device (PORT_PLAN.md section 4.5,
option A, Matt 24 Nov): the BARD queue's **FM device**, the cart's timed register writes to the YM2612 + SN76489
that play the VGM tune, on channels the cart owns, while the tune plays on the others. The music is three
**placeholder** FM tunes of our own, written by a script and replaceable later.

## The FM device (every cart can use it)

| piece | file | what |
|---|---|---|
| engine | `libs/c/vgm/zm_vgm_sfx.c` | the device's ring (512), its two clocks, the commands; hooks in `vgm_seq.c` / `vgm_stream.c` / `zm_vgm.c` |
| owners | `libs/c/vgm/zm_vgm_own.c` | which channel a register is for; each writer's register image |
| output map | `libs/c/vgm/zm_vgm_out.c` `zm_vgm_out_lead` | the output frame -> engine frame map, from the output stage's own arithmetic |
| imports | `machine/audio/vgm.zig`, `fpga/host/audio_vgm.c` | `machineVgmSfxOn / Push / Stat` (HW audio 1.4.0): the page's wasm and BARD's native C |
| queue | `libs/zig/players/fm_live.zig`, `sid_live.zig`, `vgm.zig` | addresses `$C000..$C3FF`; the VGM player plays the queue |
| exports | `apps/zig/demo_audio_sidlive.zig`, `demo_audio_vgm.zig` | the hand-over, the song room, `audioBardDevice`, `audioBardLate` |
| cart API | `libs/zig/bard_fm.zig` (`zg.bard.fm`) | `own`, `ym`, `psg`, `key`, `freq`, `patch`, `tone`, `noise`, `tuneStop` |
| capability | `machine/sdk/caps.zig` bit 6 `bard_fm` | HW 1.20.0; docs/hardware/ABI_RULES.md |

How it works:

- **Ownership.** The cart says which channels it owns (`own(t, mask)`, FM n = bit n, PSG n = bit 8 + n; the racer's
  `SFX_DEFAULT` is FM 5-6, PSG tone 3 and the noise). A cart write to a channel it does not own is refused: by
  `zg.bard.fm` at once (false, nothing queued) and by the device (counted). The tune's writes to an owned channel
  are kept aside in the tune's register image and put back when the cart lets the channel go. The cart's own writes
  are kept too and put back after a tune load resets the chips. Global registers (0x22-0x27: LFO, timers, channel
  3's mode; 0xA8-0xAE) stay the tune's; the DAC (0x2A, 0x2B) is FM 6's.
- **Latches.** The YM2612 latches A4-A6 per port and the SN76489 has one latch: a cart pair interleaved with the
  tune's would take the wrong high byte or channel. Each side has its own: the cart's A4 is kept by the device and
  swapped in for its A0 only; the cart's PSG latch likewise.
- **Time.** The queue anchors the stamps as for the SID and AY devices; then the FM writes go to the engine's ring
  at once, each with its stamp on the device's output clock. The engine applies a write before the engine frame its
  stamp maps to (`2 + stamp x rate / 44,100` after a load, from the output stage's state in general), in the order
  written. The output stage renders 256 engine frames ahead (~430 output samples), so a write must arrive that far
  ahead to land on its stamp: with a lead of >= 1024 (`fm.LEAD_MIN`) the samples do not depend on when a write came
  or on the host's render size (proved below). A write that comes later is applied at once and counted `late`.
- **Opening.** The first FM write hands the queue from the SID player to the VGM player. A VGM playing when the queue
  opens keeps playing; a VGM loaded while the device is open (or no device is picked yet) plays under the queue: the
  queue's block moved to the top of song RAM (below the Paula rings), and `audioSongCapacity` is 958,464 bytes while
  the queue is open. `fm.tuneStop(t)` stops the tune at t and the effects play on: `zg.stopSong()` would end the
  queue too (it is the machine's reset of every player), so the racer never calls it with the device open.
- **A DAC tune.** Taking FM 6 switches the DAC off (the tune's DAC writes are kept aside while the cart owns
  FM 6), so the cart's FM 6 sounds as loud over KESTREL FM's drums as over nothing.
- **A refused load** under the queue (too big, a corrupt `.vgz`) leaves the device's chips with no tune: the
  effects play on, the old tune does not sequence over the bytes just staged.
- **Off.** With the device off nothing changes: `vgm_check.mjs`'s 11 pinned tunes, the stage and the player are the
  same samples as before.
- The board's debug page says "VGM" / `fmlive` for an FM queue (`bsndinfo.h` kind 3).

## The racer's sound

`racer/sfx.zig` (pure, `sfx_test.zig`) compares the race's state with the last frame's and writes only what changed,
`racer/audio.zig` sends it:

| sound | voice | trigger | writes |
|---|---|---|---|
| engine | FM 6, algorithm 4 with feedback | keyed while the car runs (countdown, race, finish; free drive) | the pitch pair (A6, A2) when the rpm moves 6 F-number steps: `300 + rpm x 900 / max_rpm`, block 3 (61-244 Hz) |
| impact | FM 5, serial chain, full feedback, fast decay | a wall hit or a contact (`hits + contacts` grows) | key off, key on |
| skid | the noise, white, clock / 1024 | a tyre slides in the race | 2 on, 1 off |
| countdown | PSG tone 3 | entering the countdown, its ticks 30 and 60: A4; the race starts: A5 | 3 a note |
| checkpoint | PSG tone 3 | a checkpoint (the time extension) | E5 B5 |
| lap | PSG tone 3 | a lap closed | C5 E5 G5 C6 |
| finish | PSG tone 3 | a win: a six-note fanfare; a time-out: G4 E4 C4 | |

A quiet frame writes nothing; a busy one a handful (at most 24, the rest counted). The impact is keyed off after 12
frames (a tune load puts the cart's channels back keys and all, so a held key would replay the thump). The pause
(`p`) silences the effects; the tune plays on.

Stamps: the cart's own sample clock, moved on each frame by the real time since the last (`hostMicros`, a gap
counted as 200 ms at most); the queue anchors its first batch a lead (3072, 70 ms) ahead, so every write keeps
arriving ~70 ms ahead whatever the frame rate. If the clock falls within 2048 samples of the sound side's position
(drift, a stall, a harness machine with no clock) it jumps ahead to it. `racer_audio_check.mjs` pipes the cart's
ring into demo-audio's queue at 60, 30 and 20 fps with the position a frame old: nothing late, refused, dropped or
re-anchored.

## The music

`tools/racer/make_tunes.py` writes three original loops with `libs/c/vgm/vgm_write.py` (the KESTREL FM method):
`site/music/kr_title.vgm` (118 BPM, E minor), `kr_race.vgm` (150 BPM, A minor), `kr_results.vgm` (96 BPM, major
sevenths). They use **FM 1-4 and PSG tones 1-2 only** (no DAC, no LFO), so the effects' channels never cut a part of
the tune. Placeholders: M5 replaces them (the script's `--check` is in the gate).

The cue (`racer/music.zig`): the attract lap the title tune, the countdown and the race the race tune (one file for
both: the start-to-loop hand-over of the local tunes does not reload it), the finish and results the results tune,
the grid silence (`fm.tuneStop`, or `zg.stopSong` without the device). **`-Dracer_music` wins**: with it the local
tunes (Matt's Virtua Racing rips, never committed) replace the placeholders, and the effects start **off**,
because those tunes use all six FM channels, the DAC and the noise: `h` takes the channels then (the tune loses
those parts while the effects own them) and gives them back.

Without the device (`zg.caps.has(.bard_fm)` clear, or a host with no BARD queue: the gate's harness machines) the
music plays alone through `zg.requestSong` / `zg.stopSong`, as M3's local option did.

## Keys

| key | what |
|---|---|
| `z` | the music off / on |
| `h` | the effects off / on (off: the channels given back to the tune) |
| `p` | (the pause) also silences the effects |
| `j` | the benchmark laps' audio (`b`, `x`): off by default, so lap v1/v2 stay the silent runs the earlier pins and board numbers are; on: the race tune and the effects over lap v2 (the M4 bench) |

## Costs (estimates; no board run yet)

- **BARD.** The engine's work is its operators: `kr_race` alone 5,695 a 60 Hz frame (mean), with the racer's effects
  (the engine note held, impacts, beeps, noise) 8,494, the busiest frames (two 256-frame refills) 12,288. On the
  YM2612 study's whole-player numbers (Green Hill Zone 7,102 operators = 57.7 %, Special Stage 10,595 = 65.5 %;
  YM2612_BARD_STUDY.md section 6) that is **~55 % for the tune, ~61 % with the effects, ~69 % on the busiest
  frames**: under the M4 bar (BARD <= 75 % at the start grid), with the caveat that the study's calibration is two
  points and the board beat the sim by ~7 points on Sonic. The device adds a ring check a render and a few
  register writes a frame (no measurable cost). The engine note is held all race: a cheaper patch (fewer operators,
  algorithm 7 with two carriers off) is the knob if the board says otherwise.
- **snd.bin**: 2,053,712 of 2,097,152 bytes (43 KB left; +~9.6 KB).
- **The cart**: `sfx.step` + a few `zg.bard.fm` pushes in `update`, outside the stage timers: the stage times of lap
  v2 are unchanged by construction (and with `j` off the bench does no audio work beyond the cue check).

## Decisions for Matt

- **Which wins with `-Dracer_music`**: the local tunes, effects off by default (`h` turns them on, muting the tune's
  FM 5-6, PSG 3 and noise parts). Say if you would rather keep the effects on and accept the gaps.
- **A `tuneStop` still waiting when a new tune loads stops the new tune** (it is a timed command on the device). The
  racer's cues are seconds apart; a cart that stops and restarts within a lead should not use it.
- **`zg.stopSong` ends the queue** (it is the machine's player reset on both hosts). The device's `tuneStop` command
  is the way to silence the tune; a "stop the music only" host request would need a new system entry.
- **The placeholders** are deliberately plain (four FM voices, two PSG tones); M5's final tunes keep the same channel
  plan, or the effects' mask changes with them.
