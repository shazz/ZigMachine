ZIGMACHINE

A fantasy console written in Zig, running in your browser as WebAssembly.

A sandbox for coding oldskool effects with nothing but what an 80s machine gave you: palettes, scanline interrupts, a blitter, a sound chip. Inspired by WAB and CODEF by NoNameNo.

Try it

  • + / − on the monitor change channel, TV-style.
  • ↑↓←→ pick a disk in the menu, Enter boots it, Esc goes back.
  • Sound starts on your first click or key.

a .wasm or .zmd, or drop it on the monitor. It runs locally in your browser; build one with the tutorial.

The machine is sealed

The hardware is a separate binary from the software on it. The video and sound chips, the ROM (a GEM desktop) and your cart are separate wasm modules sharing one memory, talking through memory-mapped registers — the cart can't reach inside the hardware.

  • 320×200 visible, 400×280 with borders; 4 indexed planes, 256-colour palettes.
  • Per-scanline interrupts for copper bars; borders opened the honest way, by flickering the resolution register.
  • YM2149 + 4 sample channels. SNDH tunes run their own 68000 code on an emulated CPU.
  • 2 MB per cart, floppies with a FAT, ZX0-packed assets. Carts in Zig, C or Rust.
